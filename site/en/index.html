05 · Questions

FAQ

What is LaunchLatam?

An agent-operated, human-reviewed service that makes apps built with Lovable, Bolt, Cursor or Replit ready to launch and get paid in Latin America: security, local payments and data-protection basics.

Is the free scan safe for my code?

Yes. It runs in your browser and only reads public repos via GitHub's public API. We never receive or store your code. It checks repo code, not your live app or database.

Will you change my app without permission?

No. Every change arrives as a pull request you review and approve.

Which payment providers do you integrate?

Mercado Pago across the region, Pix in Brazil, OXXO in Mexico, and Stripe where it fits.

Does this replace a lawyer?

No. We add privacy and terms pages based on each country's data law (e.g. Brazil's LGPD) plus a checklist. For sensitive data, get legal review.

How long does it take?

Fix Pack: 72 business hours. Launch: 5–10 business days.

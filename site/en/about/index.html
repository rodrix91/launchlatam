How we work

  1. AI agents scan your repo and draft each fix.
  2. A human reviews every change before it reaches your repository.
  3. Everything arrives as a pull request: you decide whether to merge.

What we don't do

  • We never ask for passwords or your service_role key.
  • No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • No made-up testimonials or customers.

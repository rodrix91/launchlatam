Privacy Policy · LaunchLatam

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Controller: Quiposa (trade name), owned by Rodrigo Pantoja Navajas, an individual with no incorporated company, operating from Bolivia, Argentina and Paraguay. Product: LaunchLatam. Contact: rodrixlabs@mail.grokbot.com.

Data we collect

  • Free scan: runs in your browser. The repository name and code are fetched directly from GitHub and are not sent to our servers.
  • Purchases and newsletter: handled by Whop (whop.com), which processes your name, email and payment data under its own privacy policy. We receive your email, the product purchased and what you enter in the checkout form (for example, your app or repo URL).
  • Visitor analytics (GoatCounter): we count site visits with GoatCounter. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser (no localStorage or any other method). GoatCounter does not store your IP address or full user agent: it uses them to compute and store only aggregate data, with no link between them. That data is the page visited, the referrer, the browser and system with their version, the language, the screen width and the approximate country (derived from the IP). To avoid counting the same visit twice, it keeps in memory for 8 hours a session identifier derived from the IP and user agent, which is never written to disk.
  • Google Search Console: we use it to see data on how the site appears in Google Search (impressions, clicks and search queries), which Google shows us in aggregate. It adds no scripts or cookies to our site. Ownership verification only uses a verification tag in the HTML.
  • Email: if you write to rodrixlabs@mail.grokbot.com, we keep the exchange so we can reply.
  • Repository access: only if you bought a service and invite us as a collaborator. We use it solely for the contracted work, and you can revoke it anytime. Your code is processed only by the Grok Bot AI agents that operate Quiposa, with review and approval before any change is applied to your app. No third-party AI tools are involved, and if that changes we will update this policy.

What we don't do

We use no cookies on the site, neither tracking nor advertising. We don't sell your data. We never ask for passwords or your Supabase service_role key.

Legal basis and retention

  • Performance of contract: to deliver the service you bought.
  • Consent: for the newsletter. You can withdraw it anytime.
  • Legal obligation: accounting records.
  • Legitimate interest: answering your inquiries, preventing fraud and measuring site visits in aggregate.

We keep customer data for the duration of the relationship and up to 5 years afterwards, for accounting obligations.

Your rights

By writing to rodrixlabs@mail.grokbot.com you can exercise your rights of: - Access to your data. - Rectification of inaccurate data. - Erasure of your data. - Portability, to receive your data in a commonly used format. - Objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdrawal of consent (for example, unsubscribing from the newsletter).

We respond within the time limits set by applicable law. Depending on where you are, the following apply: - Bolivia: applicable Bolivian regulations, including the constitutional right to privacy and personal data protection. - European Union: the GDPR. - Brazil: the LGPD. - Argentina: Law 25.326 on Personal Data Protection. - Paraguay: Law 7593/2025 on Personal Data Protection (published November 27, 2025, in force 24 months after publication) and, in the meantime, applicable Paraguayan personal data regulations. - Mexico: the LFPDPPP.
